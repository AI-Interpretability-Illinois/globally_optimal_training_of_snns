\noindent Last-step accuracies on \texttt{first\_last\_xor}. Mean $\pm$ sample standard deviation over seeds $\{0,1,2\}$ (\%).
All cone solves use \texttt{--cvx\_method cvx}. $K_{\mathrm{parallel}}=2$, \texttt{last\_layer\_readout=membrane}, normalized reservoir.
CVX grids: $\beta\in\{0.01,0.1,1.0\}$, bias $=0$. SG: $200$ epochs, lr $\in\{10^{-3},5\cdot10^{-3},10^{-2},5\cdot10^{-2},10^{-1}\}$.

\paragraph{Default (bits).}
\texttt{analog\_membrane=False}: last-layer membrane is still binarized,
$D=\mathbf{1}[\mathrm{mem}-\mathrm{bias}\ge 0]$, then L1-hinge. This is the original cone column.

\paragraph{Analog.}
\texttt{--cvx\_analog\_membrane} / \texttt{--r\_cvx\_analog\_membrane}: $D$ is the analog last-layer membrane (no extra $1[\cdot]$).
R (ridge) and SG are unchanged across the two blocks: they already read analog snntorch membrane.
